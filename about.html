About

Why this app exists and how to reach me.

Purpose

The Book and Notes Vault is a small study desk for the books I am working through. I wanted a place that keeps title, author, pages, tag and date in one row, that never asks for a server, and that lets me search with a real regular expression when a plain text box is not enough.

Everything is stored in the browser with localStorage and can be exported to JSON at any time. There is no account, no tracking and no network call after the page loads.

How it is built

  • Semantic HTML5 with landmarks, headings and real labels.
  • Mobile first CSS with a table that turns into cards on small screens.
  • Vanilla ES modules: storage, validators, search, state, ui and app.
  • Regex validation on every field, plus a safe regex search box with highlighted matches.
